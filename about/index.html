Meet Ken Robertson

Ken has brokered, developed, owned and financed property, managed it for institutional investors, and served on the investment committees that approved it.

Ken has spent more than thirty years in real estate. He began in brokerage, then co-founded and ran a retail development company for nearly a decade. He went on to executive roles at institutional investment firms, with investment committee responsibility for portfolios owned by their investors.

Both vantage points come with him to private owners.

Sierra Creek is an advisory practice first. The focus is the Southern California coast, and the work extends past any one property to business development, strategic relationships and bringing the right people together.
