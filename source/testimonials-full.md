What I value most about Ken is that he looks beyond the immediate transaction. He understands the bigger picture, pays attention to the details, and works comfortably with the many professionals involved in managing our business and personal affairs.
Above all, Ken is someone I trust completely.

**Brad Samways, Owner, 1409 Glenneyre Street, Laguna Beach**
I'm very happy to share a fortuitous experience working with Ken Robertson. After a vacancy opened in my office building on Glenneyre in Laguna Beach, I initially hired a non-local broker who went five months without a single showing. Around that time, I met Ken while working on the building. When my prior agreement ended, I called him — and within one week (I kid you not) the space was leased to an excellent tenant.
Ken's attention to detail went well beyond leasing, handling insurance, rules, and many business details I wouldn't have thought of myself. I'm a blue-collar guy and proud to have a small piece of Laguna, and my advice is simple: shop local and call Ken Robertson. I'm very grateful for his work.
