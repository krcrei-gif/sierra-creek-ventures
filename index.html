Approach

Every engagement starts with what the owner wants the next ten years to look like. The property comes second.

Real estate conversations often begin with a transaction. Sierra Creek begins with what the owner is trying to accomplish. We look at the asset, the family or partnership behind it, the debt, the tenants and the timing, and lay the options out side by side.

Sometimes the right answer is a sale. Often it is a lease, a refinance, a new partner, or leaving things alone for three more years. Brokerage is one tool among several, and rarely the place to start.

The work tends to find its way to situations where the path is unclear. That is where Ken’s years on the ownership and institutional investment side are most useful.

Common questions

What kinds of property do you work with?

Coastal retail, office, multifamily, mixed-use and investment property, centered on Laguna Beach and the South Orange County coast. Unusual assets elsewhere in Southern California are welcome too.

Do you work with families who have owned for generations?

Often. Much of the work is helping a family agree on what the property is for before anyone talks about selling, leasing or borrowing.

How is this different from hiring a broker?

Brokerage begins with a transaction. Sierra Creek begins a step earlier, with whether there should be one and of what kind. If a sale or lease is the answer, Ken is a licensed California broker and Sierra Creek handles it directly.

What does an engagement look like?

It begins with a conversation about the property and what you want from it. From there Ken reviews the asset, lays out the realistic options side by side, and stays involved through whichever one you choose.

Do you invest in deals yourself?

Not as a regular practice. In the right situation Ken may act as sponsor or managing partner of an individual investment. Sierra Creek is not an investment fund and does not offer investment products.
