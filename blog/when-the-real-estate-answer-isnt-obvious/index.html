When the Real Estate Answer Isn’t Obvious

October 11, 2026 · Ken Robertson

All posts

Some properties don’t fit neatly into a conventional real estate strategy. Their history, scale, physical constraints or ownership structure call for a different starting point.

Rather than asking only what a property is worth today, we ask: Who could find enough strategic value in it to make something possible?

This brief illustrates Sierra Creek Ventures’ approach to complex and underutilized assets, using Tustin’s historic South Hangar as an example. It explores how research, potential uses, partnerships and targeted outreach can help frame a path forward.

The hangar analysis is conceptual—an illustration of our approach, rather than a client assignment.
