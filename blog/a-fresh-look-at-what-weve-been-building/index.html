A Fresh Look at What We’ve Been Building

November 17, 2026 · Ken Robertson

All posts

We’ve spent some time redesigning our websites and wanted to share the results. It’s been a fun project, and a chance to rethink how we share information, communicate what we’re working on, and stay connected with the people we work with.

Each site serves a slightly different purpose, but they’re all connected by the same idea: helping property owners and investors make informed decisions.

Sierra Creek Ventures

This is my independent advisory practice, where I work with property owners, families, and investors on real estate strategy, transactions, and other important decisions. It’s where I bring together my institutional real estate experience with the more personal, relationship-driven work I do today.

Laguna Beach, Explained and Dana Point Properties

These are free local market resources we’ve developed for owners, investors, and the broader community. They take a closer look at the neighborhoods, major projects, hospitality developments, and other changes shaping each market. They’re also a way to make the research and local insights we share with clients more accessible, useful, and current over time.

Visit Laguna Beach, Explained  ·  Visit Dana Point Properties

I hope you’ll take a look, and feel free to share these sites with anyone you think might find them interesting or useful. We always appreciate it when our work finds its way to others through the people we know.

And if there’s something you’re working on or thinking through, I’d always enjoy hearing from you.
