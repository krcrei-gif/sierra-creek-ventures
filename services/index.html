When owners call

  • Long-held and family property

    Deciding what a property should do for the family before choosing a path. Estate questions, differing priorities between siblings or generations, and decisions that have been put off for years.

  • Assets that have stopped performing

    An aging building, a weak tenant mix, or a use that no longer fits its street. What it could become, what the market will support, and whether the numbers justify the spend.

  • A loan maturity or a lease expiration

    A deadline is forcing the question. Refinance, sell, re-tenant, improve or hold, compared plainly so the choice is deliberate.

  • Capital and ownership structure

    Bringing a partner in, buying one out, recapitalizing, or reorganizing ownership among family members and investors.

  • Property that doesn't fit a template

    History, zoning, scale or physical constraints make the standard playbook useless. The question becomes what it could be worth, and to whom.

  • Opportunities and introductions

    Work that isn't tied to one address. Spotting an opening early and bringing the right owners, operators, partners and capital into the same conversation.

Once a direction is chosen, Ken stays through the execution: asset management and ownership representation, acquisitions and dispositions, leasing and tenant strategy, and coordinating lenders, partners and specialists as they are needed.

Hospitality

A close watch on the coastal hotel market.

Hotels behave differently from other property. The operator, the brand and the capital behind them matter as much as the building.

Ken follows the coastal Orange County hotel market closely, Laguna Beach in particular, and has long-standing relationships with hotel owners and investors. Hospitality is an area of knowledge and interest for the practice, and assignments are taken on selectively.

Private capital

In the right situation, advice may lead to an investment.

Sierra Creek is an advisory practice. On a selective basis, Ken may also act as the sponsor or managing partner of an individual real estate investment. His main contribution in that role would be expertise, relationships, structuring, management and execution, at times alongside his own capital, with other investors participating as limited partners.

This is an occasional role, considered one property at a time. Sierra Creek is not an investment fund and does not offer investment products, and nothing on this site is an offer to invest.
